# ADR 0013: Pivot to an AI fashion advisor

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-14

## Context

The owner wants RealDrop to be the place fashion enthusiasts go to build outfits: choose a style, recreate looks from social media, get real recommendations across brands. The price-tracking work (collectors, history, real-drop rule) is the data foundation.

## Decision

RealDrop becomes an AI fashion advisor with honest prices. Launch stays 27 Nov 2026 as an MVP: style profile, shop the look, For you feed, outfit boards, redesign; stylist chat if ready. Price honesty stays on every recommendation. Alerts, wardrobe and accounts move after launch.

## Consequences

Roadmap phases 5-10 rewritten. Catalog understanding (attributes and style tags) becomes the critical path. Product, use cases and architecture docs rewritten.
